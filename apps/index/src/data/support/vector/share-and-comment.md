Drawings are private to you until you share them. You can share with specific people, with a team, or with anyone who has a link, and choose whether they can view or edit. If you can edit a drawing, you can also leave comments, either on a single object or on the drawing as a whole.

## Share a drawing

Open the drawing, then open the sharing dialog in one of two ways:

- Click the **Share** button (the person icon) in the top-right of the toolbar.
- Open the **File** menu and choose **Share**.

### Share with specific people

1. In the sharing dialog, type a name or email address in the box at the top, then pick the person.
2. They appear under **People with access** as an **Editor** by default.
3. To change their access, open the dropdown next to their name and choose **Editor** (view and edit) or **Viewer** (view only).
4. To remove someone, set their dropdown to **Remove**.
5. Click **Save**.

### Share with a team

If you belong to a team, click **Share with team** at the bottom of the dialog and pick the team. Everyone in that team gets access.

### Share with a link

Under **General access**, a drawing starts as **Restricted**: only the people you have added can open it.

To share more widely, click the lock icon to switch it to **Unrestricted**, then choose **Can edit** or **Can view**. Use the **Copy link** button in the **People with access** header to copy the link and send it to whoever you like.

<div class="eigen-callout">

An **Unrestricted** link still requires the other person to sign in to Eigen. It isn't open to the public web.

</div>

### Let people know

Open the **File** menu and choose **Email collaborators** to write a message to everyone you have shared with. Eigen adds a link to the drawing for you. Save your sharing changes first, because the option is greyed out while you have unsaved changes.

### Control who can change sharing

If you own the drawing, you can stop editors from re-sharing it. In the sharing dialog, turn off **Editors can share**. With it off, only you can add or remove people. Editors can still work on the drawing, but they cannot change who else has access.

## Comment on an object

Comments attach to a single object on the canvas, such as a shape, an arrow, or a text box. You need editor access to add one.

1. Right-click the object.
2. Choose **Add comment** from the menu.
3. In the **New comment** dialog, give the comment a title and, if you like, a description and a colour. You can also assign it to someone.
4. Click **Add comment**.

A small coloured triangle appears in the top-right corner of the object to show it has a comment.

### Open and reply to a comment

Click the coloured triangle on an object to open that comment's thread. You can read the messages, add a reply, and mark the comment as resolved.

### View all comments

Click the **Comments** button (speech bubble icon) in the toolbar to open the comments panel on the right. The panel lists every comment on the drawing, and the **New comment** button at the top of the panel adds one that belongs to the drawing as a whole rather than to an object. Use the filter button (funnel icon) at the top of the panel to narrow the list by who a comment is **Assigned to**, by colour, or by **Status**: **Open**, **Resolved**, or **All**.

Click a comment in the panel to jump to the object it is attached to and open its thread.

### Resolve a comment

Open a comment and mark it as resolved. Resolved comments are hidden by default. Set the status filter to **Resolved** or **All** to see them again.
