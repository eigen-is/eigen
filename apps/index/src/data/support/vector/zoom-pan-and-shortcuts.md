Vector gives you an endless canvas to draw on. This page covers how to move around it, how to find text in a drawing, and every keyboard shortcut, grouped so you can look one up fast.

Shortcuts are written for macOS and for Windows and Linux. On a Mac, **⌘** is the Command key and **⇧** is Shift. On Windows and Linux, use **Ctrl** where the Mac uses **⌘**.

## Move around the canvas

You pan and zoom the canvas with the mouse or trackpad. Nothing you do here changes the drawing, so pan and zoom freely to find your place.

- **Pan** by scrolling. A scroll wheel or a two-finger trackpad swipe moves the canvas. Hold **Shift** while scrolling a wheel to pan sideways.
- **Zoom** by holding **Ctrl** or **⌘** and scrolling, or by pinching on a trackpad. The canvas zooms towards the pointer, so the spot under your cursor stays put.
- **Pan by dragging** when you'd rather not scroll. Hold the **Space** bar and drag, or drag with the middle mouse button. Both move the canvas without touching your objects.

Zoom runs from 10% right up to 3000%.

### The zoom readout

The bottom-right corner shows the current zoom as a percentage. Click it to reset the view to 100%, centred on the middle of the canvas. Its tooltip reads **Reset zoom**.

## Find text in a drawing

Press **⌘F** (or **Ctrl+F**) to open the find bar. It searches the text in every object on the canvas, including the parts you have scrolled away from.

- Type your term. Matching objects are highlighted as you type.
- Press **⌘G** for the next match and **⌘⇧G** for the previous one. The canvas moves to each match and selects it.
- Press **Esc** to close the find bar.

You can also share a link that opens a drawing with the find bar already searching. Add `?q=` and your term to the drawing's web address, for example `?q=budget`. Whoever opens the link lands on the drawing with that term found and highlighted.

## Keyboard shortcuts

### Tools

Press a letter or the matching number to pick a tool. The Image tool has no shortcut, because you add an image by uploading or pasting it.

| Action | Mac | Windows/Linux |
|---|---|---|
| Select | `V` or `1` | `V` or `1` |
| Rectangle | `R` or `2` | `R` or `2` |
| Diamond | `D` or `3` | `D` or `3` |
| Ellipse | `O` or `4` | `O` or `4` |
| Arrow | `A` or `5` | `A` or `5` |
| Line | `L` or `6` | `L` or `6` |
| Draw | `P` or `7` | `P` or `7` |
| Text | `T` or `8` | `T` or `8` |
| Eraser | `E` or `0` | `E` or `0` |
| Keep the tool active after drawing | `Q` | `Q` |

By default a tool goes back to **Select** once you've drawn a shape. Press **Q** to lock the tool on, so you can draw several of the same shape in a row.

### Editing

| Action | Mac | Windows/Linux |
|---|---|---|
| Copy | `⌘C` | `Ctrl+C` |
| Cut | `⌘X` | `Ctrl+X` |
| Paste | `⌘V` | `Ctrl+V` |
| Duplicate the selection | `⌘D` | `Ctrl+D` |
| Select everything | `⌘A` | `Ctrl+A` |
| Delete the selection | `Delete` or `Backspace` | `Delete` or `Backspace` |
| Undo | `⌘Z` | `Ctrl+Z` |
| Redo | `⌘⇧Z` or `⌘Y` | `Ctrl+Shift+Z` or `Ctrl+Y` |
| Nudge the selection by 1px | Arrow keys | Arrow keys |
| Nudge the selection further | `⇧` + arrow keys | `Shift` + arrow keys |
| Cancel the current action, or clear the selection | `Esc` | `Esc` |

Press **Esc** once to cancel a shape you're drawing or a selection you're dragging. Press it again to clear the selection, and again to return to the **Select** tool.

### Arrange

These change the stacking order of the selected objects, front to back.

| Action | Mac | Windows/Linux |
|---|---|---|
| Send backward | `⌘[` | `Ctrl+[` |
| Bring forward | `⌘]` | `Ctrl+]` |
| Send to back | `⌘⇧[` | `Ctrl+Shift+[` |
| Bring to front | `⌘⇧]` | `Ctrl+Shift+]` |

### View and find

| Action | Mac | Windows/Linux |
|---|---|---|
| Pan the canvas | Hold `Space` and drag, or middle-mouse drag | Hold `Space` and drag, or middle-mouse drag |
| Zoom in or out | `⌘` + scroll, or pinch | `Ctrl` + scroll, or pinch |
| Open the find bar | `⌘F` | `Ctrl+F` |
| Next match | `⌘G` | `Ctrl+G` |
| Previous match | `⌘⇧G` | `Ctrl+Shift+G` |
| Close the find bar | `Esc` | `Esc` |
