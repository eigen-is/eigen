You can turn any word or phrase in a document into a clickable link, or insert a URL on its own. Links open in a new tab.

## Insert a link

### On a wide screen

1. Select the text you want to turn into a link, or place your cursor where you want a new link.
2. Click the **Add link** button in the toolbar (the chain-link icon).
3. In the **Insert link** dialog, type or paste the address into **Link address**. An address without `https://` gets it added for you.
4. Press Enter or click **OK**. **OK** stays off until the address is a valid link, and **Please enter a valid link** shows under the field when it isn't.

**Display text** shows the text you selected; it keeps its formatting, so you can't change it there. With no text selected, type what the link should say in **Display text**. If you leave it empty, the address becomes the link text.

### From the Insert menu

This works on any screen.

1. Select the text you want to link, or place your cursor where you want the link.
2. Click **Insert** in the toolbar.
3. Choose **Link**.
4. Fill in the **Insert link** dialog as above, then click **OK**.

## Change the address of a link

Click anywhere inside the linked text, then click the **Add link** button or choose **Link** from the **Insert** menu. The **Edit link** dialog opens with the current address. Change it and click **OK**.

You can also right-click the link and choose **Edit link**.

## Remove a link

Right-click the link and choose **Remove link**. The whole link is removed and the text stays in place. This works on links in the main text of the document.

Anywhere else, such as in a table, click inside the link and click the **Remove link** button (the broken-chain icon) that appears in the toolbar when your cursor is on a link.

## Open or copy a link

Right-click the link and choose **Open link** to open it in a new tab, or **Copy link** to copy its address.
