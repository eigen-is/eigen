You can insert images and tables anywhere in a document. Images can come from your device or from Drive.
Tables start as a 3×3 grid and you can grow them from there.

## Insert an image

### On desktop

1. Place your cursor where you want the image to appear.
2. Click the **Insert image** button in the toolbar (the image icon with a plus symbol).
3. The **Insert image** dialog opens. Choose where to get the image from:
   - Click **Upload from device** to pick a file from your computer.
   - Or browse your Drive and click a file, then click **Select**.
4. The image appears in the document at the cursor position.

You can also drag an image file from your computer and drop it straight onto the document.

### On mobile

1. Place your cursor where you want the image.
2. Tap **Insert** in the toolbar.
3. Tap **Image**.
4. The **Insert image** dialog opens. Choose **Upload from device** to pick a file, or browse Drive and tap **Select**.

### Paste an image

If you have an image on the clipboard (copied from another app or from elsewhere in the document), paste it with Ctrl+V (or ⌘V on Mac). The image is inserted at the cursor.

## Resize and position an image

Click an image to select it. Resize handles appear at the edges. Drag a handle to change the width. The height adjusts to keep the proportions.

On a wide screen, selecting an image also opens the **Image** panel on the right side of the editor. From there you can:

- **Style**: set the image to sit on its own line (the default), float to the left, or float to the right with text wrapping around it.
- **Align**: when the image is on its own line, choose left, centre, or right alignment.
- **Alt**: add alternative text, which is used by screen readers and shown if the image fails to load.
- **Cap**: add a caption that appears beneath the image.
- **Replace image**: swap the image for a different file from your device or Drive, keeping the same position in the document.

## Insert a table

### On desktop

1. Place your cursor where you want the table.
2. Click the **Insert table** button in the toolbar (the grid icon).
3. A 3×3 table with a header row is inserted straight away.

### On mobile

1. Place your cursor where you want the table.
2. Tap **Insert** in the toolbar.
3. Tap **Table**.

## Edit a table

Click inside the table. The **Table** panel opens on the right side of the editor (on a wide screen).

**Add or remove columns**

Under **Columns**, click **Before** or **After** to add a column on either side of the one your cursor is in. Click **Delete column** to remove the current column.

**Add or remove rows**

Under **Rows**, click **Before** or **After** to add a row above or below the current one. Click **Delete row** to remove the current row.

**Merge and split cells**

Select two or more cells, then click **Merge** under **Cells** to join them into one. Click **Split** to divide a merged cell back into its original cells.

**Toggle the header row**

Click **Toggle header row** to switch the first row between a styled header row and a normal row.

**Delete the table**

Click **Delete table** at the bottom of the **Table** panel to remove the entire table from the document.
