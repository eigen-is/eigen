A table of contents lists your document's headings, each indented by its level. It keeps itself up to date: rename, add, or remove a heading and the table of contents changes with it, for everyone who has the document open.

## Add a table of contents

1. Click where the table of contents should go, in a paragraph of your text. In the middle of a paragraph, the table of contents splits it in two.
2. Open the **Insert** menu and choose **Table of contents**.

It lists your headings from **Heading 1** to **Heading 3**, and you can [choose how many levels it lists](#choose-how-many-heading-levels-it-lists). A heading with no text, the **Title**, and the **Subtitle** aren't listed. To turn a line into a heading, see [Format text](/support/docs/format-text). Until the document has headings, the table of contents shows **Headings you add appear here**.

## Choose how many heading levels it lists

1. Click to the right of the lines in the table of contents to select it. A blue outline shows around it, and the **Table of contents** panel opens on the right side of the editor.
2. Under **Heading levels**, choose a number from 1 to 6 for **Show levels**. With **2**, it lists **Heading 1** and **Heading 2**.

The change shows for everyone who has the document open, and the PDF and the Word file list the same levels. The panel opens on a wide screen, for people who can edit the document. On a phone, you can't change the levels.

## Go to a heading

Click a line in the table of contents. Your cursor moves to the start of that heading and the page scrolls to it. This works for people who can only view the document too.

## Remove a table of contents

Click to the right of the lines in the table of contents to select it. A blue outline shows around it. Press **Backspace** to remove it.

## Good to know

- The table of contents in the editor has no page numbers, because the editor shows your document as one long page. The PDF and print show each heading's page number after a row of dots.
- In the Word file, the page numbers fill in when Word updates the table of contents. The web page download lists the headings as links, without page numbers.
- A table of contents goes between paragraphs of your text. It can't go in a table, a list, a quote, a header, a footer, or a footnote, so **Table of contents** is grayed out there.
- A table of contents in a Word file you import becomes one that lists your document's headings. See [Import a Word document](/support/docs/import-word).
