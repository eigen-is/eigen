Eigen brings your mail, files, calendar, and more together in one place, each as its own app. This page shows you
how to sign in, set up your profile, and move between the apps.

## Sign in

Open Eigen in your browser and you land on the sign-in screen.

1. On the **Sign in** tab, type your username. This is the part of your email address before the `@`. Your domain
   is filled in for you, so you only type the first part.
2. Type your **Password**.
3. Click **Sign in**.

That's it. You arrive at your apps, ready to work.

## Set up your profile

Once you're in, take a minute to set up your account in **Space**, your own corner of Eigen. Open it from your
picture in the top-right corner, or use the links below.

- Add your name and a photo on the [**Personal info**](/space/user) page.
- Make your account safer: [**Change password**](/space/security/password) and turn on
  [**Two factor authentication**](/space/security/2fa).
- Set up [**Integrations**](/space/services) if you want to use Eigen with other apps on your computer, such as a
  desktop mail client or your computer's file browser.

<div class="eigen-callout">

Your name and picture are public to everyone else in your Eigen. They show up next to your messages, on files you
share, and wherever you take part, so it's worth setting a name and photo you're happy for others to see.

</div>

## Move between the apps

Eigen is a set of apps that share the same account and the same look. At the top left of every app is a grid
button (its label is **Switch app**). Click it to open the full list and jump to any of them:

- **Mail** for your inbox.
- **Drive** for your files and folders.
- **Calendar** for your schedule.
- **Docs**, **Sheets**, and **Slides** for documents, spreadsheets, and presentations.
- **Stickies** for notes and boards, and **Chat** for messages.
- **Contacts** for the people you know.

The app you're in is highlighted in the list, so you always know where you are.

In a hurry? Press ⌘K (or Ctrl+K on Windows) to open a search box that jumps straight to any app, file, or
message. See [Find anything with search and the command palette](/support/getting-started/search-and-command-palette).
