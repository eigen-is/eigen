You can delete any card you no longer need. Deletion is permanent, but you can undo it straight away if
you change your mind.

You need write access to the board to delete cards. The option does not appear if you are a viewer.

## Delete a card

1. Right-click the card on the board to open the context menu.
2. Click **Delete sticky**.
3. A confirmation dialog appears with the title **Delete Card**. It warns you that the deletion is permanent.
4. Click **Delete** to confirm, or **Cancel** to go back.

The card is removed from the board immediately.

## Undo a deletion

Press **Ctrl+Z** (Windows) or **Cmd+Z** (macOS) straight after deleting to bring the card back. The undo
button in the toolbar does the same thing.

<div class="eigen-callout">

Undo only works in your current session. If you close the board and reopen it, the deletion cannot be undone.

</div>
