A Stickies board is private to you until you share it. You can share with specific people, with a whole team,
or with anyone who has the link, and choose whether they can view or also make changes.

## Open the Share dialog

You can open the **Share** dialog from two places:

- **From inside the board**: click the share button (person-with-plus icon) in the top-right corner of the toolbar.
  It is only visible when you have write access.
- **From the board list**: right-click a board (or click its **⋮** menu), open the **Share** submenu, and
  choose **Share**.

## Share with specific people

1. In the **Share** dialog, type an email address or name in the field at the top.
2. Select the person from the suggestions. They appear under **People with access**.
3. New people are added as **Editor** by default. To change that, click the dropdown next to their name and
   choose **Viewer** (view only) or **Editor** (view and make changes).
4. Click **Save**.

To remove someone later, open the same dialog, set their dropdown to **Remove**, and click **Save**.

## Share with a team

If you belong to a team, click **Share with team** at the bottom of the dialog and pick the team. Everyone
in that team gets access.

## Share with a link

Under **General access**, a board starts as **Restricted**. Only the people you have added can open it.

To share more widely, click the lock icon to switch it to **Unrestricted**, then choose **Can edit** or
**Can view**. Click **Save**, then use the link icon in the **Share** dialog to copy the link.

<div class="eigen-callout">

An **Unrestricted** link still requires the other person to sign in to Eigen. It is not open to the public web.
There is no anonymous, sign-in-free sharing.

</div>

## Let people know

After you save your changes, you can send everyone you have shared with a short message and the link.
Open the **File** menu in the board toolbar and choose **Email collaborators**.

## See boards others have shared with you

Open the sidebar and look under **Sharing**. Click **Stickies shared with me** to see every board someone
else has given you access to.

To see the boards you have shared, click **Stickies shared by me**.

## Control who can change sharing

If you own the board, you can stop editors from re-sharing it. In the **Share** dialog, turn off
**Editors can share**. With it off, only you can add or remove people. Editors can still work on the board,
but they cannot change who else has access.

When sharing is restricted, editors who open the **Share** dialog see a read-only list of who has access and
cannot make changes.
