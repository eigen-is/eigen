A card can carry files: a screenshot of the bug, the PDF you are reviewing, the document the work is written
in. Everyone with access to the board can open them. You need write access to add one.

## Attach a file

1. Open the card and click the pencil icon (**Edit**), or start a new card with **Add a sticky**.
2. Click the paperclip button (**Add attachment**) in the bottom-left corner of the dialog.
3. The **Attach file** dialog opens on your Drive. Click the file you want, or hold Shift or Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac)
   to pick several.
4. Click **Select**. The button shows a count, such as **Select (2)**, when you pick more than one.
5. The files appear as chips in the dialog. Click **Save**, or **Add Sticky** on a new card, to attach them.

To use a file that isn't in Drive yet, click **Upload from device** at the bottom of the **Attach file** dialog
and pick the files in the chooser that opens.

You can also drag files from your desktop straight onto the open dialog, or paste an image you have copied. Both
add a chip the same way.

Nothing is attached until you save the card. **Cancel** leaves the card as it was.

## What gets copied and what gets linked

A document, spreadsheet, presentation, or other Eigen file attaches as a link rather than as a copy. Its chip
reads **Open [filename]** and opens the file in a new tab, and the other person needs access to that file to
open it.

Everything else, such as a PDF or an image, is attached as a copy, so it stays with the card even if the
original in Drive changes or goes away.

## Remove an attachment

Open the card and click the pencil icon (**Edit**), then click the **X** on the chip of the file you want to
take off. Click **Save**. The rest stay attached.

## What the card shows

Attachments give the card face two things you can read at a glance:

- Attach an image, and the card shows it as a cover picture across the top.
- A paperclip icon with a number tells you how many files are on the card.

## Open an attachment

Click the card to open it. The chips sit below the description. Click one to open a preview of the file.

Right-click a chip for an uploaded file, or press and hold it on a phone, to see what else you can do with that one
file: **Quick preview**, **Download**, and **Save to Drive…**. An Excel or Word file also offers to convert it, a
contact file offers **Import to Contacts**, a saved email offers **Import to Mail**, and a calendar file offers
**Import to Calendar**. When you convert one, Eigen asks for a Drive folder first, saves the file
there, and creates the converted copy beside it when you click **Save and convert**. The new spreadsheet or document
opens straight away, so you leave the board.
