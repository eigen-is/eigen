Eigen keeps a record of what happens to your files: who created one, who edited it, who changed the sharing, and
who left a comment. You can read that record in Drive, or in more detail inside an editor.

## Recent activity in Drive

1. Open Drive and click a file or folder to select it.
2. Look at the detail panel on the right, under **Shared with**.

The **Recent activity** block lists the five most recent things that happened. Each line shows the person's
avatar, what they did, and how long ago. Your own actions are shown as **You**.

For a folder, the list covers everything inside it as well, so you can see activity on a whole project without
opening each file.

The block only appears once there is something to show. A file nobody has touched since it arrived has no
**Recent activity** section.

## What the lines mean

The wording follows the action:

- **created** or **uploaded**, with the file size for an upload.
- **edited**, for a change inside a document, spreadsheet, presentation, board, or drawing.
- **renamed**, with the old and new name.
- **moved**, **copied**, **trashed**, or **deleted**.
- **updated sharing**, with the people added or removed underneath.
- **restored a version**, with the name of the version.
- **commented**, with a short preview of the comment.
- **added a card**, **moved a card**, or **removed a card**, for a board in Stickies.

Click a line to go to what it describes. A comment line opens that comment, a card line opens that card, a
sharing line opens the **Share** dialog, and the rest open the file.

## The Activity panel in an editor

Docs, Sheets, Slides, Stickies, and Vector have a fuller view of the same record.

1. Open the file in its editor.
2. Click the **Activity** button in the toolbar. The toolbar buttons are icons, and each one shows its name when
   you point at it. On a narrow screen, open the **⋮** menu in the toolbar and choose **Activity**.

The panel opens beside the document and lists up to 50 entries, newest first. Lines that refer to a comment or a
card open it in the document when you click them. If nothing has happened yet, the panel says **No activity
yet**.

Click **Activity** again to close the panel.

<div class="eigen-callout">

Activity is a record of what people did. To go back to an earlier state of the file, use version history
instead. See [See and restore previous versions of a file](/support/drive/file-versions).

</div>

## Keep up without checking

If you would rather be told than go and look, watch the file or the folder it lives in. See
[Watch a file or folder for changes](/support/drive/watch-for-changes).
