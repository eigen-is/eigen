Everything in Drive is private to you until you share it. You can share a file or folder with specific people, with a whole team, or with anyone who has the link, and decide whether they can view it or also make changes.

## Share with specific people

1. Find the file or folder in Drive.
2. Right-click it (or open the **⋮** menu), hover over **Share**, then click **Share** in the sub-menu that appears.
3. In the **Share** dialog, type a name or email address in the box at the top, then pick the person. They appear under **People with access**.
4. Choose what they can do from the dropdown next to their name: **Editor** (view and make changes) or **Viewer** (view only). New people are added as **Editors**.
5. Click **Save**. An address still in the box is added first, so you don't lose it.

To remove someone later, open the same dialog, set their dropdown to **Remove**, and click **Save**. Changed your mind before saving? Type their address again to add them back.

## Share with a team

If you belong to a team, click **Share with team** at the bottom of the dialog and pick the team. Everyone in that team gets access. On a phone the button shows only its people icon.

## Share with a link

Under **General access**, a file starts as **Restricted**. Only the people you've added can open it.

To share more widely, pick another choice from the dropdown under **General access**:

- **Anyone signed in with the link**: anyone who signs in to Eigen and has the link can open it. Choose **Can view** or **Can edit** beside it. With **Can edit**, people who get in through the link can change the file or folder, but not who has access. Only the owner and people added by name, or through a team, can do that.
- **Anyone with the link**: anyone who has the link can view it, with no account and no sign-in. [Share with a public link](/support/drive/share-with-a-public-link) explains what they see.

Use the **Copy link** button to copy the link, then click **Save** and send the link to whoever you like.

With **Anyone signed in with the link**, the other person still signs in to Eigen. Only **Anyone with the link** opens a file or folder to someone without an account, and only to view it.

## Let people know

In the **Share** dialog, click the **Email collaborators** button to open a compose window where you can write a message to everyone you've shared with. Eigen includes a link to the file automatically. The button is disabled while you have unsaved changes, so save first if you've just added someone.

## Control who can change sharing

If you own the file, you can stop editors from re-sharing it. In the **Share** dialog, turn off **Editors can share**. With it off, only you can add or remove people. Editors can still work on the file, but they can't change who else has access.
