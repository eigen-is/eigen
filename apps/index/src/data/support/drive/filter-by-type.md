Drive shows you your files inside folders, but sometimes you want to see all your images, or all your chats,
without hunting through every folder. The **Filters** section in the sidebar collects every file of a given
type across all your drives in one list.

## Open a type filter

1. Open Drive.
2. In the left sidebar, scroll to the **Filters** section.
3. Click one of the filter rows:
   - **All images**: shows every image file across your drives.
   - **All docs**: opens Docs and lists your documents there.
   - **All stickies**: opens Stickies and lists your boards there.
   - **All chats**: shows every chat in your Drive.
   - **All slides**: opens Slides and lists your presentations there.
   - **All sheets**: opens Sheets and lists your spreadsheets there.

## Work with files in a filtered view

The file list works the same way as the regular folder view. Click a file to open the detail panel on the
right, or double-click to open it. Right-click a file (or click its **⋮** button) for options such as
**Download**, **Share**, **Quick preview**, and **Move to trash**.

You cannot upload files or create folders from a filtered view. To add or organise files, click **Drive** at
the top of the sidebar to go back to your folder view.

## Return to your folders

Click **Drive** at the top of the sidebar to leave the filtered view and go back to your files and folders.
