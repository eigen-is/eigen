Chat lets you send messages to other people in Eigen, organise conversations into named rooms, and share files
without leaving the browser. This page is a quick tour of what you can do.

## Your chat rooms

All your rooms are listed in the sidebar on the left. Each room shows a small indicator dot when there are unread
messages. Chat automatically marks a room as read when you open it.

The sidebar is split into two areas:

- Your personal rooms are at the top. You create these yourself and share them with whoever you want.
- **Team Chats** appears below a divider if you belong to any teams that have chat rooms. Team rooms are shared
  with everyone in that team.

## Create a room

Click **New chat** at the top of the sidebar. Give the room a name and choose where to save it. The new room
opens straight away.

If you have no rooms yet, Chat shows a **Create your first chat** button in the centre of the screen.

## Send a message

Type in the box at the bottom of the room and press Enter to send. Shift+Enter adds a new line without sending.

URLs and email addresses in messages are turned into links automatically.

## Mention someone

Type `@` followed by a name or email address. A list of room members appears. Select the person you want from
the list to complete the mention.

## Attach a file

Click the paperclip button next to the message box. You can pick a file from Drive or upload one from your
computer. You can also paste an image directly into the message box.

## Edit or delete a message

Hover over one of your messages. An action bar appears in the top-right corner of the message. Click the pencil
icon to edit the text, or the bin icon to delete the message.

## Personal and team chats

Personal rooms are ones you create in your own Drive. You control who can access them. Team rooms belong to a
team and are visible to all team members automatically.

See [Personal chats and team chats](/support/chat/personal-and-team-chats) for a fuller explanation of the
difference.

## Slash commands

Chat has a set of slash commands you can type in the message box. Type `/` to see a suggestion list, or send
`/help` to print all available commands in the room. Commands include:

- `/whisper [User] [Message]` to send a private message only that person can see.
- `/me [action]` to post an emote in italics.
- `/invite [User]` to invite someone to the room.

See [Chat commands](/support/chat/commands) for the full list.
