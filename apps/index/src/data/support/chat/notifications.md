Chat tells you about new messages in two ways: unread dots on room names in the sidebar, and notifications in
the bell menu at the top of the screen.

## Unread dots in the sidebar

When a room has new messages since you last opened it, a small dot appears on the room's icon in the sidebar.
This works for both your personal rooms and for rooms listed under **Team Chats**.

Opening the room clears the dot. Chat marks the room as read automatically as soon as you view it.

## The notification bell

The bell icon in the top bar shows a count when you have unread notifications. The count covers all Eigen apps,
not Chat alone.

Click the bell to open the **Notifications** panel. Each notification shows the sender's avatar, a title, a
short preview of the message, and how long ago it arrived. Unread notifications appear with a highlighted
background.

From the panel you can:

- Click a notification to go straight to the message in Chat.
- Click **Mark all read** to clear the count at once.
- Click the **✕** button on an individual notification to dismiss it.

## What triggers a notification

**@mention:** When someone types your email address in a message, you receive a notification titled "You were
mentioned in …".

**New message in a room:** If you have previously sent a message in a room, or if you own it, you receive a
"New message in …" notification when someone else posts there.

**Whisper:** If someone sends you a private whisper, you receive a notification the same way you would for a
regular message.

You do not receive a notification for your own messages.

## Toast pop-ups

When a notification arrives while you are using Eigen, a small pop-up appears in the bottom-right corner of the screen.
It shows the title and a short preview. Click **View** to go directly to the message.

## Browser tab title

When you have unread notifications, the page title in the browser tab starts with a count in brackets, for
example `(3) eigen|chat>`. This lets you see new activity even when the tab is in the background.
