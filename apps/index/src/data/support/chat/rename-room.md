You can rename a chat room at any time from inside the room.

## Rename a room

1. Open the room in Chat.
2. Click the pencil icon in the toolbar at the top of the room. The tooltip reads **Edit**.
3. In the rename dialog, edit the name in the field and type the new one.
4. Click **Rename**.

The new name appears straight away in the toolbar and in the sidebar.

## Who can rename a room

You need edit access to the room to rename it. If you only have viewer access, the rename will not be saved and an error will appear.
