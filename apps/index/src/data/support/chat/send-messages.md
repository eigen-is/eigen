You can send a message in any chat room you have access to, and edit or delete your own messages afterwards.

## Send a message

1. Open the chat room from the sidebar.
2. Click in the text box at the bottom of the room. It shows a placeholder like "Message My Room".
3. Type your message.
4. Press **Enter** to send, or click the send button on the right.

To add a new line without sending, press **Shift+Enter**.

URLs and email addresses you type are turned into links automatically.

## Edit a message

You can only edit messages you sent yourself.

1. Hover over the message. A small action bar appears in the top-right corner of it.
2. Click the pencil icon (**Edit**).
3. The message text becomes editable. Change the text as needed.
4. Press **Enter** to save, or click **Save**. Press **Escape** or click **Cancel** to discard your changes.

Once a message has been edited, it shows "(edited)" next to the timestamp.

You can also press the **up arrow** key in an empty message box to start editing your most recent message straight away.

## Delete a message

You can only delete messages you sent yourself.

1. Hover over the message. A small action bar appears in the top-right corner of it.
2. Click the bin icon (**Delete**).
3. A confirmation dialog appears. Click **Delete** to confirm.

Deleted messages are removed permanently and cannot be recovered.
