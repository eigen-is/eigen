When you are done with a message, you can keep it out of the way without losing it, throw it away, or flag it as
junk. This page covers all three: **Archive**, **Delete**, and **Report Spam**.

Each action has two ways in. Open a message and use the buttons in the toolbar above it, or right-click a message
in the list and pick the action from the menu. The same actions also live in the **More actions** menu (the **⋮**
button) above an open message.

## Archive a message

Archiving moves a message out of your Inbox and into the **Archive** mailbox. It stays there for as long as you
want, so this is the way to keep something without leaving it in your Inbox.

1. Open the message, or right-click it in the list.
2. Click the **Archive** button in the toolbar, or choose **Archive** from the menu.

The message leaves the list straight away and appears under **Archive** in the sidebar. The **Archive** button is
hidden when you are already looking at a message in the Archive, since there is nowhere left to move it.

To bring an archived message back, open **Archive** in the sidebar and move the message to another mailbox. See
[Move email between folders](/support/mail/move-email-between-folders).

## Report spam

If a message is junk, report it. **Report Spam** moves it to your **Spam** mailbox and takes it out of the Inbox.

1. Open the message, or right-click it in the list.
2. Click the **Report Spam** button in the toolbar, or choose **Report Spam** from the menu.

The **Report Spam** button is hidden when you are already in the **Spam** mailbox. If a real message ends up in
Spam by mistake, open **Spam** in the sidebar and move it back to your Inbox.

## Delete a message

Deleting a message sends it to the **Trash**.

1. Open the message, or right-click it in the list.
2. Click the **Delete** button in the toolbar, or choose **Delete** from the menu.

A deleted message is not gone for good. It sits in **Trash**, where you can still read it or move it back to
another mailbox if you deleted it by mistake.

### Delete a message for good

To remove a message permanently, open **Trash** in the sidebar and delete it from there. Because this can't be
undone, Mail asks you to confirm first.

1. Open **Trash** in the sidebar.
2. Open or right-click the message, then choose **Delete**.
3. In the **Delete Email** dialog, check the subject, then click **Delete** to confirm.

<div class="eigen-callout">

Deleting from any other mailbox only moves the message to **Trash**, so you can get it back. Deleting from inside
**Trash** removes it for good. There is no undo for that step.

</div>

## Act on several messages at once

To archive, delete, or report several messages together, select them in the list first, then right-click any one
of them and choose the action. The menu shows the count, for example **Archive 3 emails**. See
[Work with several emails at once](/support/mail/work-with-several-emails).
