When someone sends you a file, you can keep it in Drive rather than in your mailbox. Attachments appear as chips
above the message in the reading pane, and each one can go to a folder of your choosing in one step.

## Save one attachment

1. Open the message.
2. Click the chip for the file you want.
3. In the **Save attachment** dialog, browse to the folder in Drive where you want it, then click **Save here**.

## Save all of them at once

1. Open the message.
2. Click the down-arrow icon to the right of the chips (**Save attachments**).
3. Browse to the folder you want in the **Save attachments** dialog, then click **Save here**.

Every attachment on the message is saved to that folder together. On a message with one attachment the button and
the dialog read **Save attachment** instead.

## Download to your computer instead

If you would rather have the file on your own machine, click **Download instead** in the bottom-left corner of the
same dialog. The dialog closes and your browser downloads the files. Nothing is written to Drive.
