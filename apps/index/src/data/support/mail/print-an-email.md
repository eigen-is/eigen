You can print any single email in Mail, or save it as a PDF, from the message you are reading. Mail prints the open message on its own, without the sidebar, the list, or the toolbar around it.

## Print the message you're reading

1. Open the message you want to print. It appears in the reading pane on the right.
2. Click the **More actions** menu (the **⋮** button) in the toolbar above the message.
3. Choose **Print**.
4. Your browser's print dialog opens. Pick your printer and settings, then click **Print**.

The printed page shows the subject, who the message is from and to, the date, the list of attachments, and the full message. The rest of the screen, like the mailbox list and the toolbar, is left out.

<div class="eigen-callout">

**Print** is on the open message, not the list. To print one email, open it first, then use the **More actions** menu. Right-clicking a message in the list does not offer **Print**.

</div>

## Save an email as a PDF

There is no separate "save as PDF" button. The same print dialog does it.

1. Follow the steps above to open the print dialog.
2. Where the dialog asks for a destination or printer, choose **Save as PDF** (the exact wording depends on your browser).
3. Click **Save** and pick where to keep the file.

If you want the original email file rather than a printout, use **Download** in the same **More actions** menu instead. See [Download an email](/support/mail/download-an-email).
