Select an object on a slide and the panel on the right fills with its style controls. This page lists every section, top to bottom, and what each control does.

Which sections you see depends on the object. **Stroke**, **Sketch**, and **Appearance** show for everything. **Fill** shows for shapes and text boxes, not for images or arrows. The **Text**, **Image**, and arrow sections each show only for their own kind of object. With more than one object selected, you see the controls they share.

With nothing selected, the panel shows the slide background instead. See [Slide backgrounds](/support/slides/slide-backgrounds).

## Transform

Position, size, and rotation, in slide units.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **X**, **Y** | The top-left corner's position |
| **W**, **H** | Width and height |
| **Keep aspect ratio** | Locks width and height together when you resize |
| **Angle** | Rotation in degrees |

## Text

Shows for a text box. Controls the type itself; the box behind it uses the **Fill** and **Stroke** sections below.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Font** | The typeface |
| **Size** | Font size in points |
| **Color** | Text colour |
| **Style** | **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, and **Strikethrough** |
| **Align** | Left, centre, right, or justified |
| **Vertical** | Top, middle, or bottom of the box |

### Spacing

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Letter** | Space between letters |
| **Line** | Line height |
| **Padding** | Inset between the text and the box edge |

## Image

Shows for an image. Sets how the picture sits in its box.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Fit** | Fits the whole picture inside the box |
| **Fill** | Fills the box, cropping the overflow |
| **Stretch** | Stretches the picture to the box, ignoring its proportions |

## Arrows

An arrow has its own sections in place of **Fill**. If the arrow has a label, a **Text** section with **Font** and **Size** appears above them.

**Arrow**:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Type** | **Sharp**, **Curved**, or **Elbow** |
| **Edges** | For elbow arrows, **Sharp** or **Rounded** corners |

**Arrowheads**:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Start** | The head at the start: **None**, **Arrow**, **Triangle**, **Bar**, or **Circle** |
| **End** | The head at the end, with the same options |

## Fill

The colour inside a shape or text box. Set a solid colour or a two-stop gradient, or turn the fill off.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Style** | How the fill is drawn: **Solid**, **Hachure**, **Cross-hatch**, or **Zigzag** |

The hatch styles draw the fill as loose pen strokes rather than a flat block, to match the hand-drawn look.

## Stroke

The outline of the object.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Color** | Outline colour |
| **Width** | Line thickness |
| **Style** | **Solid**, **Dashed**, or **Dotted** |

## Shape

Shows for shapes and images that carry a corner or edge treatment.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Corners** | **Straight**, **Curved**, or **Round** corners |
| **Edges** | For lines, **Sharp** or **Rounded** |

## Sketch

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Style** | How rough the drawing looks: **Architect** (clean), **Artist**, or **Cartoonist** (loosest) |

Every object is drawn with a hand-sketched line. This setting controls how much it wobbles.

## Appearance

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Opacity** | How see-through the object is, from 0 to 100 |

## Arrange and align

Below the style sections you can layer objects front to back and, with two or more selected, line them up. See [Arrange and align objects](/support/slides/arrange-and-align).
