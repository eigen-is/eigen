Presentations are private to you until you share them. You can share with specific people, with a team, or with anyone who has a link, and choose whether they can view or edit. If you have editor access, you can also leave comments tied to specific objects on a slide.

## Share a presentation

Open the presentation, then open the sharing dialog in one of two ways:

- Click the **Share** button in the top-right corner of the toolbar.
- Open the **File** menu and choose **Share**.

### Share with specific people

1. In the sharing dialog, type a name or email address in the box at the top, then select the person.
2. They appear under **People with access** as an **Editor** by default.
3. To change their level of access, open the dropdown next to their name and choose **Editor** (can view and edit) or **Viewer** (can view only).
4. To remove someone, set their dropdown to **Remove**.
5. Click **Save**.

### Share with a team

If you belong to a team, click **Share with team** at the bottom of the dialog and pick the team. Every member of that team gets access.

### Share with a link

Under **General access**, a presentation starts as **Restricted**: only the people you have added can open it.

To share more widely, click the lock icon to switch it to **Unrestricted**, then choose **Can edit** or **Can view** from the dropdown. Use the copy-link button (the chain-link icon) in the **People with access** header to copy the link and send it to whoever you like.

<div class="eigen-callout">

An **Unrestricted** link still requires the other person to sign in to Eigen. It isn't open to the public web.

</div>

### Let people know

After you save, click the email button (envelope icon) in the **People with access** header to open the **Email collaborators** dialog. The button is greyed out until you have saved your changes.

### Control who can change sharing

If you own the presentation, you can stop editors from re-sharing it. In the sharing dialog, turn off **Editors can share**. With it off, only you can add or remove people. Editors can still work on the presentation, but they cannot change who else has access.

## Comment on an object

Comments attach to individual text boxes or images. You need editor access to add a comment.

1. Right-click a text box or image on the canvas.
2. Choose **Add comment** from the context menu.
3. A **New comment** dialog appears. Give the comment a title and, optionally, a description and colour.
4. Click **Add comment**.

A small coloured triangle appears in the top-right corner of the object to show it has a comment.

### Open and reply to a comment

Click the coloured triangle on an object to open that comment's thread. You can read the messages, add a reply, and mark the comment as resolved.

### View all comments

Click the **Comments** button (speech bubble icon) in the toolbar to open the comments panel on the right. The panel lists every comment on the current presentation. Use the **All** and **For you** tabs to filter by audience, and the status dropdown to show **Open**, **Resolved**, or **All** comments.

Click a comment in the panel to jump to the object it is attached to and open its thread.

### Resolve a comment

Open the comment thread and mark it as resolved. Resolved comments are hidden by default in the panel. Switch the status filter to **Resolved** or **All** to see them again.
