Presentations are private to you until you share them. You can share with specific people, with a team, or with anyone who has a link, and choose whether they can view or edit. If you have editor access, you can also leave comments tied to specific objects on a slide.

## Share a presentation

Open the presentation, then open the sharing dialog in one of two ways:

- Click the **Share** button in the top-right corner of the toolbar.
- Open the **File** menu and choose **Share**.

### Share with specific people

1. In the sharing dialog, type a name or email address in the box at the top, then select the person.
2. They appear under **People with access** as an **Editor** by default.
3. To change their level of access, open the dropdown next to their name and choose **Editor** (can view and edit) or **Viewer** (can view only).
4. To remove someone, set their dropdown to **Remove**.
5. Click **Save**.

### Share with a team

If you belong to a team, click **Share with team** at the bottom of the dialog and pick the team. Every member of that team gets access.

### Share with a link

Under **General access**, a presentation starts as **Restricted**: only the people you have added can open it.

To share more widely, pick another choice from the dropdown under **General access**:

- **Anyone signed in with the link**: anyone who signs in to Eigen and has the link can open the presentation. Choose **Can view** or **Can edit** beside it. With **Can edit**, people who get in through the link can change the presentation, but not who has access. Only the owner and people added by name, or through a team, can do that.
- **Anyone with the link**: anyone who has the link can view the presentation, with no account and no sign-in ([Share with a public link](/support/drive/share-with-a-public-link)).

Use the copy-link button (the chain-link icon) in the **People with access** header to copy the link. Then click **Save**, and send the link to whoever you like.

With **Anyone signed in with the link**, the other person still signs in to Eigen. Only **Anyone with the link** opens the presentation to someone without an account, and only to view it.

### Let people know

After you save, click the email button (envelope icon) in the **People with access** header to open the **Email collaborators** dialog. The button is grayed out until you have saved your changes.

### Control who can change sharing

If you own the presentation, you can stop editors from re-sharing it. In the sharing dialog, turn off **Editors can share**. With it off, only you can add or remove people. Editors can still work on the presentation, but they cannot change who else has access.

## Comment on an object

Comments attach to a single object on a slide, such as a text box, an image, a shape, or an arrow. You need editor access to add a comment.

1. Right-click the object on the canvas.
2. Choose **Add comment** from the context menu.
3. A **New comment** dialog appears. Give the comment a title and, optionally, a description and color.
4. Click **Add comment**.

Or select one object and choose **Comment** from the **Insert** menu, which opens the same dialog.

A small colored triangle appears in the top-right corner of the object to show it has a comment.

### Open and reply to a comment

Click the colored triangle on an object to open that comment's thread. You can read the messages, add a reply, and mark the comment as resolved.

To read a comment without opening it, rest your mouse on the triangle. The comment's card appears beside it, and a click on the card opens the thread.

### View all comments

Click the **Comments** button (speech bubble icon) in the toolbar to open the comments panel on the right. The panel lists every comment on the current presentation. Use the filter button (funnel icon) at the top of the panel to narrow the list by who a comment is **Assigned to**, by color, or by **Status**: **Open**, **Resolved**, or **All**.

Click a comment in the panel to jump to the object it is attached to and open its thread.

A red dot on a comment means it has something new for you: a reply in a thread you've written in, a mention, or an assignment. Opening the comment clears the dot.

### Resolve a comment

Open the comment thread and mark it as resolved. Resolved comments are hidden by default in the panel. Switch the status filter to **Resolved** or **All** to see them again.

### Copy a link to a comment

Open a comment and click the link icon (**Copy link**) near the top of the dialog. The link opens the presentation with that comment's thread already visible.

If the comment is deleted later, the link still opens it, but only its title shows, grayed out, with **This comment was deleted** below it. Its replies are gone, and it can't be replied to, resolved or assigned. The same goes for a notification about it.
