Every new slide starts plain white. You can give a slide a solid colour, a gradient, or an image behind your text and pictures, and choose whether the change lands on one slide, the slides that follow, or the whole presentation.

You need editor access to change a background. If you can only view the presentation, the controls below are not available.

## Open the background options

1. Open your presentation in [Slides](/slides) and select the slide you want in the left panel.
2. Click an empty part of the canvas so nothing is selected, or press **Escape** to clear the selection.
3. The properties panel on the right now shows the **Background** section for that slide, with a row of buttons for the background type: no fill, solid colour, gradient, and image.

Whatever you set here changes the current slide straight away. To spread it to other slides, use **Apply to** at the bottom, described below.

## Set a solid colour

1. Click the solid colour button (the palette icon) in the **Background** row.
2. Click the swatch next to **Color** and pick a colour from the picker. The slide updates as you choose.

## Set a gradient

1. Click the gradient button in the **Background** row.
2. Set the two ends of the gradient with the **From** and **To** swatches. Either one can be set to **Transparent**.
3. Pick a direction from the grid under **Direction**. Each button points the gradient a different way, from **Top-left** through to **Bottom-right**.

## Set an image

1. Click the image button (the picture icon) in the **Background** row. The **Background image** picker opens.
2. Choose your image one of two ways:
   - Click **Upload from device** to pick a file from your computer.
   - Browse your Drive, click an image, then click **Select**.
3. The image fills the slide. Use the **Fit** dropdown to choose how it sits: **Cover** fills the slide and crops if needed, and **Contain** shows the whole image.
4. To swap it for a different one, click **Replace**.

## Apply to more than one slide

By default a background change affects only the current slide. To reuse it across the presentation:

1. Set the background you want on the current slide.
2. Open the **Apply to** dropdown and choose **This slide**, **This and following**, or **All slides**.
3. Click **Apply**. The background copies to every slide in the range you picked.

Applying to all slides is a deliberate step, so dragging a colour never changes the whole presentation by accident.

## Remove a background

To return a slide to plain white, click the no fill button (the crossed-out circle) at the start of the **Background** row. To remove an image but keep a background type set, click the bin icon next to **Replace**.

To clear the background from several slides at once, remove it on one slide, then choose the range in **Apply to** and click **Apply**.
