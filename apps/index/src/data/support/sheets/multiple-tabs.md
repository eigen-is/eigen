A spreadsheet can contain as many sheets as you need. Each sheet has its own tab along the bottom of the editor.
You can add, rename, reorder, and colour-code them to keep your data organised.

## Add a sheet

Click the **+** button at the left of the tab bar. The new sheet is added at the end of the tab bar.

You can also go to **Insert → Sheet** in the menu bar.

## Switch between sheets

Click any tab to switch to that sheet.

If you have many sheets and the tabs overflow the bar, scroll left or right using the arrow buttons that appear at
the right of the tab bar.

To see all sheets at once, click the grid icon (next to the **+** button) to open the sheet list, then
click the sheet you want.

## Rename a sheet

Double-click a tab to edit its name inline. Type the new name, then press **Enter** or click anywhere else to save.

You can also click the **⌄** arrow on the tab and choose **Rename** from the menu.

## Reorder sheets

Drag a tab left or right and drop it in the new position.

You can also click the **⌄** arrow on a tab and choose **Move left** or **Move right** to shift it one place at
a time.

## Copy a sheet

Click the **⌄** arrow on a tab and choose **Copy**. A duplicate of the sheet appears next to the original.

## Add a colour to a tab

Click the **⌄** arrow on a tab and choose **Change color**. Pick a colour from the palette. A thin stripe of
that colour appears along the bottom of the tab.

To clear the colour, open the same menu, choose **Change color** again, and click **Reset color**.

## Hide and show a sheet

To hide a sheet, click the **⌄** arrow on its tab and choose **Hide**. The tab disappears from the bar. You
cannot hide the last visible sheet.

To show a hidden sheet again, open the **View** menu in the menu bar. If any sheets are hidden, a **Hidden
sheets** submenu appears. Click the sheet name to make it visible again.

## Delete a sheet

Click the **⌄** arrow on a tab and choose **Delete**. You will be asked to confirm before the sheet is removed.

You cannot delete the last sheet in a spreadsheet.
