You can download a copy of any spreadsheet to your computer without removing it from Eigen. Three formats are
available: Excel, PDF, and web page.

## Download a spreadsheet

1. Open the spreadsheet in Sheets.
2. Click **File** in the top-left of the menu bar.
3. Hover over **Download** to open the submenu.
4. Choose the format you want:
   - **Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)** saves the workbook in the standard Excel format. All sheets are included.
   - **PDF (.pdf)** saves each sheet as a page in a single PDF document.
   - **Web Page (.html)** saves the spreadsheet as an HTML file you can open in a browser.
5. An **Exporting document** progress bar appears while Eigen prepares the file. When it is ready, your browser
   downloads it automatically.

The downloaded file takes the name of the spreadsheet.

## What carries over to Excel

When you download as **Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)**, the following are preserved:

- Cell values: text, numbers, booleans, dates, and formulas
- Number formats
- Merged cells
- Column widths and row heights
- Cell borders
- Text formatting: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, font size, text colour, and fill colour
- Text alignment, text wrapping, and text rotation
- The sheet tab names and their order
- Hidden gridlines (if you hid them)

<div class="eigen-callout">

Conditional formatting rules and data validation rules are not included in the Excel export. If you need
those, keep the spreadsheet in Sheets where they apply.

</div>

## What to use each format for

- **Excel**: share with people who use Microsoft Excel or another spreadsheet app, or keep an offline backup
  you can edit elsewhere.
- **PDF**: send a version people cannot easily change, such as a report or a table for a presentation.
- **Web Page**: embed the spreadsheet in a web page or archive its appearance at a point in time.

There is no CSV export. To get data out as CSV, download as Excel and then save as CSV from Excel or another
spreadsheet app.
