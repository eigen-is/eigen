Calendar has single-key shortcuts in the style of Google Calendar: **t** goes to today, **w** shows the week, **c** creates an event. They are on from the start, with nothing to turn on. This page lists every key.

<div class="eigen-callout">

The keys go quiet while a dialog is open, such as **New event**, while a menu or the view list is open, while you type in a field, and while you work in an event's details panel. So typing an **m** in a title never switches the view.

</div>

## Views

Each view key keeps the day you are looking at.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **d** | Day |
| **x** | 3 days |
| **w** | Week |
| **m** | Month |
| **a** | Schedule |

## Navigation

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **t** | Today, in the view you are in |
| **j** or **Right arrow** | Next day, 3 days, week, month or four weeks |
| **k** or **Left arrow** | Previous day, 3 days, week, month or four weeks |

## Actions

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **c** | New event, at the next quarter hour of today, an hour long |

A key pressed with **⌘**, **Ctrl**, **Alt** or **Shift** held does nothing in Calendar, so the browser's own shortcuts keep working.
