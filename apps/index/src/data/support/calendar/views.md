Calendar opens in the week view on a computer and in the 3-day view on a phone. You can switch to a single day, 3 days, a week, a whole month or a schedule list, and use the navigation controls in the toolbar to move between periods.

## Switch views

On a computer, pick **Day**, **3 days**, **Week**, **Month** or **Schedule** from the dropdown on the right side of the toolbar. On a phone, tap one in the sidebar, where the view you are in is highlighted.

The view changes immediately and stays on the day you were looking at. If you are looking at 18 June in the month view, switching to the week view shows the week of 18 June. The 3-day view starts on that day and shows the two days after it.

The schedule view starts on that day too and shows the next four weeks as a list. Only days with events appear, each with its events in time order: the time, or **All day**, the title and the place. Click an event to see its details. With nothing in those four weeks, the schedule says **Nothing planned**.

## Read the day, 3-day and week views

These views show the hours of the day down the left side, with a column for each day. Each event is a block that starts and ends at its times, so you can see how long it lasts and where the gaps are. A block shows the title, then the time, then the place, as far as its height allows; a short block shows the title and start time on one line. Events at the same time sit side by side. Click an event to see its details.

All-day events, and events that last 24 hours or more, sit in a strip above the hours, as one bar across the days they cover. The strip is three rows high. On a day with more than three, it shows two and counts the rest: click the count, such as **2 more**, or the **Show all** arrow on the left, to see them all.

A line marks the current time across today's column, with the time in the hour column beside it. The view opens an hour before the current time when today is in it, and at 07:00 otherwise. Weekends are slightly shaded. Click a date at the top of a column to open that day on its own.

## Read the month view

The month view shows every week of the month as a row, with each day's events under its date. An event that lasts several days is one bar across those days; when it runs into the next week, it continues on the next row. A timed event shows a colored dot, its start time and its title. On a phone, or in a narrow window, the time is left out so the title still fits.

Each day shows as many events as fit, so a larger window shows more. When a day has more events than fit, its last line counts the rest, such as **3 more**. Click it to see all of that day's events in a list, and click an event in the list to see its details. Click anywhere else to close the list.

Click a date number to open that day in the day view.

## Move to the next or previous period

Use the arrow buttons in the toolbar to step through time:

- The **left arrow** moves back by the length of the view: one day, 3 days, one week, one month or, in the schedule, four weeks.
- The **right arrow** moves forward by the same amount.

In the month view the arrows keep the day of the month, so 31 January steps to 28 February.

The title next to the arrows updates to show where you are. In the month view it reads something like **June 2026**. In the day view it shows the date, for example **Tuesday, 2 Jun 2026**. In the week, 3-day and schedule views it shows the date range, for example **1 – 7 Jun 2026**.

## Jump to today

Click **Today** in the toolbar to return to today in the view you are in.

Today's date is always highlighted with a filled circle, so you can spot it at a glance.

On a phone, **Today** folds into the **⋮** button (**View options**) on the right of the toolbar.

## Pick a day in the mini month

The sidebar shows a small month under **Create event**, starting on Monday. Today has a filled circle, and the days the view shows are shaded, such as the whole week in the week view. Use the arrows beside the month name to look at other months; the view stays where it is until you click a day. Click a day to open it in the view you are in: in the week view, the week of that day. On a phone, the click also takes you from the sidebar back to the calendar.

## Use the keyboard

On a computer, single keys do the same as the toolbar: **t** for today, **j** and **k** for the next and previous period, and **d**, **x**, **w**, **m** and **a** for the views. See [Keyboard shortcuts in Calendar](/support/calendar/keyboard-shortcuts) for every key.

## Create an event by clicking the grid

In the month view, you can click an empty part of any day to open the event creation dialog with that date pre-filled. In the day, 3-day and week views, click an empty time to open it at that time, or drag over a range of time with a mouse to fill in the start and the end. See [Create and edit an event](/support/calendar/create-event) for the full steps.

With a mouse or a pen you can also drag an existing event to another time or day, or drag its bottom edge to change when it ends. See [Move an event by dragging](/support/calendar/create-event#move-an-event-by-dragging).
