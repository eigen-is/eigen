Calendar is where you keep your schedule in Eigen. You can create events, invite other people, and see calendars that
others have shared with you. This page is a quick tour of the layout and the things you'll do most.

## The two views

Calendar opens in the **Month** view. Each cell in the grid is a day, and your events appear inside it. Today's date
is highlighted with a filled circle.

To switch to the week grid, open the dropdown in the top-right corner of the toolbar and pick **Week**. The week
view shows seven columns, one per day, with events listed in time order inside each column. All-day events appear at
the top of the column.

The dropdown remembers your choice as you navigate.

## Navigate between dates

The toolbar at the top has three controls for moving around:

- Click **Today** to jump back to the current date.
- Click the left or right chevron buttons to move one month (or one week) backwards or forwards.

The title in the toolbar updates to show the period you're looking at: a month name and year in the month view, or
the date range of the week in the week view.

## Your calendars

The sidebar lists the calendars you own under **My Calendars**. Each calendar has a colour-coded checkbox next to its
name. Tick or untick the checkbox to show or hide that calendar's events in the grid.

If someone has shared a calendar with you, it appears under **Shared with me**. Team calendars show up under
**Team Calendars**. You can show or hide these the same way.

## Create an event

There are two ways to open the **New Event** dialog:

- Click **Create event** at the top of the sidebar.
- Click directly on a day in the grid. The dialog opens with that date already filled in.

In the dialog:

1. Type a title in the **Add title** field at the top.
2. Set the date and time. To make it an all-day event, tick **All day** and the time pickers are replaced with a
   date range.
3. If the event repeats, open the recurrence dropdown (it says **Does not repeat** by default) and choose an option
   such as **Daily**, **Weekly on [day]**, or **Every weekday (Monday to Friday)**.
4. To invite people, type a name or email address into the **Add guests** field and click the **+** button. Each
   person appears in the list below with a **Pending** status until they respond.
5. Add an optional **Add location** or **Add description**.
6. If you have more than one calendar, use the calendar picker at the bottom to choose which calendar the event
   belongs to.
7. Click **Save**.

The event appears in the grid straight away. Click it to see its details.
