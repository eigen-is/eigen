Calendar is where you keep your schedule in Eigen. You create events, invite other people, and see your team's calendars and the ones others share with you beside your own.

The screen has two parts: the sidebar on the left, with **Create event** at the top and your calendars below it, and the grid on the right.

:::

## The views

Calendar opens in the **Week** view on a computer and in the **3 days** view on a phone. The week view shows seven columns, Monday to Sunday, with the hours of the day running down the side. Each event is a block as long as it lasts, and all-day events sit in a strip above the hours. Today's date is highlighted with a filled circle.

To switch on a computer, pick **Day**, **3 days**, **Week**, **Month** or **Schedule** from the dropdown in the top-right corner of the toolbar. On a phone, tap one in the sidebar. In the **Month** view each cell in the grid is a day, and your events appear inside it. See [Switch views and navigate dates](/support/calendar/views) for more.

## Navigate between dates

The toolbar at the top has three controls for moving around:

- Click **Today** to jump back to the current date.
- Click the left or right chevron buttons to move one view's length backwards or forwards: a day, 3 days, a week, a month or the schedule's four weeks.

The title in the toolbar updates to show the period you're looking at: a month name and year in the month view, the date in the day view, or the range of dates in the other views.

## Your calendars

The sidebar lists the calendars you own under **My calendars**. Each calendar has a color-coded checkbox next to its name. Tick or untick the checkbox to show or hide that calendar's events in the grid.

If someone has shared a calendar with you, it appears under **Shared with me**. Team calendars show up under **Team calendars**. You can show or hide these the same way.

## Create an event

There are two ways to open the **New event** dialog:

- Click **Create event** at the top of the sidebar.
- Click an empty time in the grid, or drag over a range of time with a mouse. The dialog opens with that time already filled in. In the **Month** view, click a day to fill in its date.

In the dialog:

1. Type a title in the **Add title** field at the top.
2. Set the date and time. To make it an all-day event, tick **All day** and the time pickers are replaced with a date range.
3. If the event repeats, open the recurrence dropdown (it says **Does not repeat** by default) and choose an option such as **Daily**, **Weekly on [day]**, or **Every weekday (Monday to Friday)**.
4. To invite people, type a name or email address into the **Add guests** field and click the **+** button. Each person appears in the list below with a **Pending** status until they respond.
5. Add an optional **Add location** or **Add description**.
6. If you have more than one calendar, use the calendar picker at the bottom to choose which calendar the event belongs to.
7. Click **Save**.

The event appears in the grid straight away. Click it to see its details.
