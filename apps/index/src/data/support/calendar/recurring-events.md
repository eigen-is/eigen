A recurring event is one that repeats on a schedule. You set it up once and Calendar generates all the
occurrences automatically. You can later edit or delete a single occurrence, all future ones from a
given date, or the whole series.

## Set a recurrence when creating an event

1. Open the **New Event** form by clicking **Create event** in the sidebar, or by clicking any day in
   the calendar view.
2. Fill in the title, date, and time as normal.
3. In the row below the time pickers, open the recurrence dropdown. It shows **Does not repeat** by
   default.
4. Choose how often you want the event to repeat:

   - **Daily**: every day from the start date.
   - **Weekly on [day]**: every week on the same day as the start date.
   - **Monthly on the [ordinal] [day]**: for example, "monthly on the second Tuesday".
   - **Annually on [month] [day]**: the same date each year.
   - **Every weekday (Monday to Friday)**: each working day.

   The day and month labels in the options come from the start date you set. If you change the start
   date, open the dropdown again to see the updated options.

5. Click **Save**.

## Change the recurrence on an existing event

1. Click the recurring event in the calendar.
2. Click the pencil icon in the detail panel.
3. In the **Edit Event** dialog, open the recurrence dropdown and choose a different option.
4. Click **Save**. Calendar asks which occurrences to update:

   - **This event**: changes the recurrence for this occurrence only.
   - **This and following events**: updates this occurrence and all future ones.
   - **All events in series**: changes the whole series.

5. Click **OK**.

## Edit one occurrence without changing the others

1. Click the specific occurrence you want to change.
2. Click the pencil icon and make your changes (you can adjust the time, title, location, or any other
   field).
3. Click **Save**, then choose **This event** when Calendar asks which occurrences to update.
4. Click **OK**.

That occurrence is now separate from the rest of the series. All other occurrences are unchanged.

## Remove an occurrence or cancel the whole series

1. Click the occurrence you want to delete.
2. Click the bin icon in the detail panel.
3. Confirm the deletion scope:

   - **This event**: removes this occurrence only.
   - **This and following events**: removes this occurrence and all that come after it.
   - **All events in series**: deletes every occurrence of the recurring event.

4. Click **OK**.
