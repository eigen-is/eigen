Labels let you group contacts however you like. You might use them for work, personal, or project-based
groupings. Once a contact has a label you can click that label in the sidebar to see only those contacts.

## Create a label

Labels live in the sidebar under the **Labels** heading.

1. Open Contacts.
2. Click the **+** button next to **Labels** in the sidebar. The **Add Label** dialog opens.
3. Enter a name in the **Label Name** field.
4. Click the colour swatch to pick a colour for the label.
5. Click **Save**. The new label appears in the sidebar.

## Assign a label to a contact

You can assign labels when you create a contact or at any time by editing one.

1. Open the contact and click the pencil icon (**Edit**) in the toolbar.
2. In the **Labels** row under **Basic Information**, select a label from the **Add label** dropdown.
3. Repeat to add more labels. Each label appears as a coloured badge.
4. Click **Save**.

To remove a label from a contact, click the small trash icon on the label's badge, then click **Save**.

### Assign from the context menu

You can assign or remove labels without opening the edit form.

1. Right-click a contact in the list. A menu appears.
2. Choose **Assign label** to open the label submenu.
3. Click a label name to toggle it on or off. A tick shows which labels are already assigned.

You can select several contacts first (hold Ctrl or Cmd while clicking) and assign a label to all of them at once.

### Drag a contact onto a label

You can also drag a contact row and drop it onto a label name in the sidebar. The label is added to that contact straight away. This works with a multi-contact selection too.

## Filter contacts by label

Click a label name in the sidebar. The list shows only contacts with that label. The label name and its colour appear above the list.

To go back to all contacts, click **My Contacts** in the sidebar.

## Edit or delete a label

To rename a label or change its colour, hover over it in the sidebar and click the pencil icon that appears on the right. The **Edit Label** dialog opens. Make your changes and click **Save**.

To delete a label, open **Edit Label** and click **Delete**. You will be asked to confirm. Deleting a label removes it from all contacts that had it, but the contacts themselves are not deleted.
