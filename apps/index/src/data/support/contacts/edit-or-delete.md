You can update any of a contact's details at any time, and delete contacts you no longer need.

## Edit a contact

1. Open Contacts and click the contact you want to change. Their details appear on the right.
2. Click the pencil icon (**Edit**) in the toolbar.
3. Make your changes. The form is divided into three sections:
   - **Basic Information**: **First name**, **Last name**, **Company**, **Job title**, and **Labels**.
   - **Contact Information**: **Email Addresses**, **Phone Numbers**, and **Addresses**.
   - **Additional Information**: **Birthday** and **Notes**.
4. Click **Save**. You are returned to the contact's detail view.

To cancel without saving, click **Cancel**.

### Add or remove a photo

In the edit form, click the camera button on the avatar to open a small menu. Choose **Upload from files** to pick an image from your computer. If the contact already has a photo, the menu also shows **Remove avatar**.

### Add extra email addresses, phone numbers, or addresses

Each of those fields has an **Add** button. Click it to add another entry. To remove one you no longer need, click the trash icon next to it. (The trash icon only appears when there are two or more entries, so the last one cannot be removed.)

### Change labels

In the **Labels** row you can pick a label from the dropdown. To remove a label, click the small trash icon on its badge.

## Delete a contact

1. Open the contact you want to delete.
2. Click the trash icon (**Delete**) in the toolbar.
3. A confirmation dialog appears. Click **Delete** to confirm, or **Cancel** to go back.

You can also right-click any contact in the list and choose **Delete** from the menu that appears.

<div class="eigen-callout">

Deleting a contact is permanent. There is no way to recover a deleted contact.

</div>
