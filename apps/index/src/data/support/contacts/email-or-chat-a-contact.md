Contacts can open Mail or Chat for you, so you never have to copy an address across by hand. Both actions work
on a single contact or on a whole selection.

## Send an email

1. Open Contacts and find the person you want to write to.
2. Right-click their row, or hover over it and click the **⋮** button.
3. Click **Send email**.

Mail opens a new message with the contact already filled in as the recipient.

You can do the same from a contact's own page. Click the contact to open their details, then use the **⋮**
button in the toolbar.

Contacts uses the first email address on the card. If a contact has no email address at all, **Send email**
is not shown.

## Start a chat

1. Right-click a contact, or open their details and click the **⋮** button in the toolbar.
2. Click **Start chat**.

If you already have a chat with that one person, and you can write in it, that chat opens. Otherwise the
**New chat** dialog opens with the person already added, so you can check the name and where the room is saved
before you create it. See [Create a chat room](/support/chat/create-room) for what the dialog does.

## Reach several people at once

Select more than one contact first, then right-click one of the selected rows. The two menu items count your
selection: **Send email to 3 contacts** and **Start chat with 3 contacts**.

- **Send email to 3 contacts** opens one new message addressed to all of them.
- **Start chat with 3 contacts** opens the **New chat** dialog with all of them added, so you get one room with
  everyone in it.

The number counts the people in your selection who have an email address. See
[Work with several contacts at once](/support/contacts/work-with-several-contacts) for how to build the
selection.

## Your own card is left out

Contacts keeps a card for you, linked to your Eigen account. Both actions drop it from the list, because an
email or a chat with yourself is not useful. If your own card is the only thing you selected, neither item
appears in the menu.

## Team members

The same two actions work for the people in your teams. Click a team in the sidebar, click a member to open
their details, then use the **⋮** button in the toolbar.
