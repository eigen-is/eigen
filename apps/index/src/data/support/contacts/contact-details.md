Contacts can hold more than a name and email address. You can attach a photo, record where someone works,
store their birthday and postal address, and add free-form notes. All of these fields are optional and can
be filled in when you first create a contact or added later.

To open the edit form for an existing contact, click the contact in the list, then click the pencil icon
(**Edit**) in the toolbar. To fill in the fields while creating a new contact, open the form with **Create
contact** in the sidebar.

## Add a photo

The avatar circle sits at the top of the edit form. Click the camera button at the bottom right of the
circle. A small menu opens with two options:

- **Upload from files**: opens a file picker so you can choose an image from your computer.
- **Remove avatar**: removes the current photo (this option only appears when a photo is already set).

After you pick a file, the image uploads and the preview updates straight away. The photo is saved when
you click **Save**.

## Workplace details

In the **Basic Information** section:

- **Company**: the organisation this person works for.
- **Job title**: their role at that organisation.

Both fields appear together in the contact detail view, shown as "Job title at Company".

## Labels

Labels let you group contacts into categories. In the **Labels** row, pick a label from the dropdown to
add it to this contact. A label appears as a coloured badge. To remove it, click the small trash icon on
the badge. You can apply more than one label to the same contact.

Labels must exist before you can assign them. See
[Organise contacts with labels and groups](/support/contacts/labels-and-groups) for how to create them.

## Postal addresses

In the **Contact Information** section, find the **Addresses** area. An empty address block is shown by
default. Fill in as many of these sub-fields as you know:

- **Street**
- **City**
- **State**
- **Postal code**
- **Country**

To add a second address, click **Add** next to the **Addresses** heading. Each address block shows a
trash icon when there is more than one, so you can remove any you no longer need.

## Birthday

In the **Additional Information** section, click the **Birthday** field and pick a date. The birthday
appears in the contact detail view with a calendar icon.

## Notes

The **Notes** field in the **Additional Information** section is a free-text area. Use it to record anything
else you want to remember: how you met, preferred contact method, or any other context. Notes appear in the
contact detail view below the other information.

## Save your changes

Click **Save** at the bottom of the form when you are done. You are taken back to the contact detail view
where the updated information is shown. To discard your changes, click **Cancel**.
