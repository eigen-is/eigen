Eigen has a small surprise for when you finish something. Celebrations are on until you turn them off, and the setting is yours alone.

## When Eigen celebrates

Open a card in Stickies, or a comment card in another app, and tick the boxes of its checklist. When you tick the last open box, a burst of confetti sometimes comes out of it. It happens about one time in three, so it stays a surprise.

A few things never celebrate:

- A checklist with only one item.
- Ticking the last box a second time while the card stays open.
- A box a teammate ticks. Only your own tick counts.

If your computer is set to reduce motion, there is no confetti. The box you ticked glows briefly instead.

## Turn celebrations off or on

1. Open [**Personal info**](/space/user) in Space.
2. Under **Preferences**, turn the **Celebrations** switch off, or on again.

The change is saved straight away and applies in every Eigen app.
