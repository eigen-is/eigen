Your name and profile picture appear to other people across Eigen: in shared documents, messages, and workspaces. You can update both at any time from the [**Personal info**](/space/user) page in Space.

## Change your name

1. Open [**Personal info**](/space/user) in Space.
2. Update the **First Name** and **Last Name** fields.
3. Click **Save**.

The first name is required. The last name is optional.

## Change your profile picture

1. Open [**Personal info**](/space/user) in Space.
2. Click the camera button in the bottom-right corner of your profile picture.
3. Choose **Upload from files** and pick an image from your computer.
4. Click **Save**.

Your new picture appears straight away in the preview. It is not saved until you click **Save**.

## Remove your profile picture

1. Open [**Personal info**](/space/user) in Space.
2. Click the camera button in the bottom-right corner of your profile picture.
3. Choose **Remove avatar**.
4. Click **Save**.

<div class="eigen-callout">

Your name and profile picture are visible to other users. They may appear in shared workspaces, messages, and documents throughout Eigen.

</div>
