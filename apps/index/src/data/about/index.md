Eigen is a self-hosted alternative to Google Workspace. It is ten apps for the work a team does together, on a server you run yourself.

You can try it without installing anything at [demo.eigen.is](https://demo.eigen.is). It is a shared workspace that resets every hour.

## The apps

You write a plan, run the numbers and sketch the site. You put it on slides and talk it over. The people you work with are in the same file at the same time, and you see what they type as they type it.

The films on these pages follow one crew: the volunteers behind the Tuimel Festival, a small arts and music festival that is made up. It is three weeks away. Their production plan, their budget and their site plan are the files you see, and the demo holds the same ones.

The apps share one login and one look, so once you know one, you know them all. The last row is for whoever runs the server.
