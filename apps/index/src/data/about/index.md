Eigen is a self-hosted alternative to Google Workspace. Simple and secure. You control your data. Your mail, files, calendar, and more live in one place. It syncs with your own apps over IMAP, SMTP, CalDAV, CardDAV, and WebDAV, and it opens and saves Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files.

You can try it without installing anything at [demo.eigen.is](https://demo.eigen.is). It is a shared workspace that resets every hour.

## The apps

Write a plan, work out the budget and draw a map of the festival grounds. Turn it into slides and talk it through with your team. Everyone can work in the same file at the same time, and you see what others type while they type it.

These pages follow one team: the volunteers who organise the Tuimel Festival, a small arts and music festival. The festival is not real, and it is always three weeks away. The files you see are the team's plan, budget and map, and you find the same files in the [demo](https://demo.eigen.is).

All the apps use one login and look alike, so once you know one, you know them all.
