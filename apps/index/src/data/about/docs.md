eigen|docs⟩ is the [word processor](/support/docs/get-started) in Eigen. You write on a page, and it saves as you type, so there is no Save button. Other people can write in the same document at the same time.

::: video src="/data/about/media/docs-hero" caption="A line added to the production plan, and undo takes it back."
:::

## Writing together

[Share a document](/support/docs/share-a-document) with people, a team or a link. Everyone with edit access can type in it at the same time, and you see [their cursor with their name on it](/support/docs/get-started).

The festival crew writes its production plan this way. The plan says they are short on crew for the build weekend. Nour opens it while Saar is reading, and adds that a new call-out goes out today. Saar sees the sentence appear on her own screen as Nour types it.

[Save a version](/support/docs/doc-versions) before a big rewrite, and you can roll back to it later.

::: video src="/data/about/media/docs-collab-pair" caption="Nour answers the crew shortage on the right; Saar sees it arrive on the left."
:::

## Comments

Select a passage and [add a comment](/support/docs/comments). It gets a highlight and its own thread, where people reply, @mention each other and resolve it when it is done. Rest your mouse on a highlight and the comment shows without opening anything.

A thread keeps a question beside the words it is about. In the plan, Joris asks on the shuttle buses whether the last one leaves at 01:00 or 02:00, because the closing set depends on it. Mees answers 02:00, and that he will put it on the info page. The question is settled, and the answer stays where the next reader finds it.

## More than text

There are [tables, images and charts](/support/docs/images-and-tables), [headers with page numbers](/support/docs/headers-and-footers) and [footnotes that number themselves](/support/docs/footnotes). The production plan has its timeline in a table, a photo of the camping field, Timo's power sum for Saturday night as an equation and a footnote on the last bus home.

[Equations](/support/docs/equations) you type in LaTeX, with a preview as you go.

## Word files

You can [bring a `.docx` in](/support/docs/import-word). The import keeps the text and formatting, tables, images, charts, footnotes and equations. You [download any document](/support/docs/export) as Word, PDF or a web page.
