eigen|contacts⟩ is the address book in Eigen. It keeps names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, birthdays and notes, and an email address on a card starts a new mail in one click ([Get started with Contacts](/support/contacts/get-started)).

## Your book and your team's

My contacts holds the people you add yourself, and one card that is you. Each team you are in has its own list of members beside it, which the team's admin keeps up to date, so nobody types the crew in twice ([Get started with Contacts](/support/contacts/get-started)). The search bar narrows either list by first name, last name or email address as you type ([Search your contacts](/support/contacts/search-contacts)).

## Cards and labels

A card holds a photo, a company and job title, postal addresses, a birthday and notes ([Add a photo, birthday, and other details](/support/contacts/contact-details)). Labels group people however you like, and a label in the sidebar shows only its people ([Organize contacts with labels](/support/contacts/labels-and-groups)). From a card, or a selection of them, you start an email or a chat with those people ([Email or chat with a contact](/support/contacts/email-or-chat-a-contact)).

## vCard files in and out

A `.vcf` file from another address book imports from your computer or from Drive, and one that came as an attachment imports straight from the mail. Contacts you already have are skipped ([Import contacts from a vCard file](/support/contacts/import-contacts)). When you leave, the whole book downloads as one file ([Export contacts as a vCard file](/support/contacts/export-contacts)).

## On your phone

Your contacts sync over CardDAV with Apple Contacts on a Mac or an iPhone and with DAVx5 on Android, both ways, photos and labels included ([Set up Eigen Contacts in a contacts app](/support/connect/contacts-client)).
