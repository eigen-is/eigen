eigen|contacts⟩ is the [address book](/support/contacts/get-started) in Eigen. It keeps names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, birthdays and notes, and an email address on a card starts a new mail in one click.

::: video src="/data/about/media/contacts-hero" caption="Saar searches the crew's address book and opens a card."
:::

## Your book and your team's

My contacts holds the people you add yourself, and one card that is you. Each team you are in has [its own list of members](/support/contacts/get-started) beside it, which the team's admin keeps up to date, so nobody types the crew in twice. The search bar [narrows either list](/support/contacts/search-contacts) by first name, last name or email address as you type.

## Cards and labels

A card holds [a photo](/support/contacts/contact-details), a company and job title, postal addresses, a birthday and notes. [Labels](/support/contacts/labels-and-groups) group people however you like, and a label in the sidebar shows only its people. From a card, or a selection of them, you [start an email or a chat](/support/contacts/email-or-chat-a-contact) with those people.

## vCard files in and out

A `.vcf` file from another address book [imports from your computer or from Drive](/support/contacts/import-contacts), and one that came as an attachment imports straight from the mail. Contacts you already have are skipped. When you leave, the whole book [downloads as one file](/support/contacts/export-contacts).

## On your phone

Your contacts [sync over CardDAV](/support/connect/contacts-client) with Apple Contacts on a Mac or an iPhone and with DAVx5 on Android, both ways, photos and labels included.
