eigen|contacts⟩ is [where you keep the people you work with](/support/contacts/get-started) in Eigen. It holds names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, birthdays and notes, and an email address on a card starts a new mail in one click.

## Your book and your team's

My contacts holds the people you add yourself, and one card that is you. Saar keeps the farmer who owns the festival field there, with her phone number and a note on the gate code.

Each team you are in has [its own list of members](/support/contacts/get-started) beside it, which the team's admin keeps up to date, so nobody types the crew in twice. The search bar [narrows either list](/support/contacts/search-contacts) by first name, last name or email address as you type.

## Cards and labels

A card holds [a photo](/support/contacts/contact-details), a company and job title, postal addresses, a birthday and notes. [Labels](/support/contacts/labels-and-groups) group people however you like, and a label in the sidebar shows only its people.

From a card, or a selection of them, you [start an email or a chat](/support/contacts/email-or-chat-a-contact) with those people. Select the stage crew, and a mail to all of them is a right-click away.

## vCard files in and out

A card is also a file you pass on. When the tent hire sent a quote, Saar put their vCard in the thread on the crew's board, so Timo could call them about sizes.

A `.vcf` file from another address book [imports from your computer or from Drive](/support/contacts/import-contacts), and one that came as an attachment imports straight from the mail. Contacts you already have are skipped. The whole book [downloads as one file](/support/contacts/export-contacts), for a backup or for another address book.

## On your phone

Your contacts [sync over CardDAV](/support/connect/contacts-client) with Apple Contacts on a Mac or an iPhone and with DAVx5 on Android, both ways, photos and labels included.
