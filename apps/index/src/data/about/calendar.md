eigen|calendar⟩ is the calendar app in Eigen. It opens on the month, with a week view one click away, and your own calendars sit beside your team's in the sidebar ([Get started with Calendar](/support/calendar/get-started)).

## More than one calendar

Everyone has a calendar of their own, and a team has one its members share. Each has a colored box in the sidebar that shows or hides its events, so you can look at the festival without your own shifts, or the other way round ([Show or hide calendars](/support/calendar/show-hide-calendars)). You can share one of your calendars with people or a team, to edit, to view, or to see only when you are busy ([Share a calendar](/support/calendar/share-calendar)). An event repeats daily, weekly or on weekdays, and you change one date or the whole series ([Create a recurring event](/support/calendar/recurring-events)).

## Guests and their answers

Add guests to an event by name or email address, and each one shows Pending until they answer Accepted, Maybe or Declined. Above three guests the list folds into a count and a summary of the replies ([Invite people and manage attendees](/support/calendar/invite-people)). When someone invites you, the event shows up in your calendar with Accept, Maybe and Decline at the bottom ([Respond to an invitation](/support/calendar/respond-to-invitation)).

## People outside Eigen

You can invite any email address. Someone on Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook gets an invitation email their app understands, and their answer comes back and updates your event. An invitation from them arrives in Mail as a card and goes into your calendar, as long as your server can verify who sent it. This needs an Eigen that hosts your email ([How invitations work with people outside Eigen](/support/calendar/external-invitations)).

## On your phone

Your calendars sync over CalDAV with Apple Calendar on a Mac or an iPhone, with Thunderbird, and with other CalDAV apps. Only the calendars you own sync; a team calendar or one shared with you stays in the browser ([Set up Eigen Calendar in a calendar client](/support/connect/calendar-client)). You can also move a calendar in or out as an `.ics` file ([Import events from a calendar file](/support/calendar/import-events), [Export a calendar or an event as a calendar file](/support/calendar/export-events)).
