eigen|sheets⟩ is the spreadsheet app in Eigen. You type a formula, the totals update, and everyone with the sheet open sees it happen. It saves as you work, so there is no Save button.

::: video src="/data/about/media/sheets-hero" caption="A formula typed, the totals update, and undo takes it back."
:::

## Working together

Several people can [edit the same sheet](/support/sheets/get-started) at the same time. Their changes merge as they type, so nobody overwrites anyone.

The festival has one budget. Bram keeps it, and Anouk has it open too. She clicks the Security line, and Bram sees which cell she is in. She types a new amount, and it is on his screen too.

You [share a sheet](/support/sheets/share-and-comment) with people, a team or a link, and you can leave a comment on a cell without touching its value.

::: video src="/data/about/media/sheets-collab-pair" caption="Two people in one budget. Anouk changes Security on the right; Bram sees it arrive on the left."
:::

## Formulas and Excel files

[Formulas](/support/sheets/formulas) work the way you expect: type `=`, pick a function from the autocomplete, click cells to add them. Bram turns the Security line into guards times hours times rate, `=14*40*16`, and the total costs and the margin follow.

[Conditional formatting](/support/sheets/conditional-formatting) and [data validation](/support/sheets/data-validation) keep a sheet readable when many people fill it in. In the budget every amount has a bar as long as it is big, and the margin turns red below zero. The status of a line comes from a list: confirmed, pending or paid.

You can [bring your `.xlsx` files](/support/sheets/import-excel). An import keeps the values, formulas, formatting, charts and images. The generator hire mailed its quote as an Excel file, and Saar opened it as a sheet in the team drive. When you leave, you [download the sheet](/support/sheets/export-spreadsheet) as Excel, PDF or a web page.

## Charts

Select a table and [insert a chart](/support/sheets/add-a-chart): column, bar, line or pie. The chart reads its cells, so it changes as soon as a number does. In the budget a column chart of the costs sits beside the table. Bram raises the stage and tech line, and its bar grows.
