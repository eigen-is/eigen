eigen|slides⟩ is the presentation app in Eigen. You build a deck slide by slide, with the slides in a rail on the left and the one you edit in the middle. When it is done, you present it from the same browser tab ([Get started](/support/slides/get-started)).

::: video src="/data/about/media/slides-hero" caption="A line on the sponsor deck changed, and undo takes it back."
:::

## Building a deck

A slide holds text, images, shapes and arrows. Double-click a text box to type in it ([Insert text and images](/support/slides/insert-content), [Draw shapes, lines, and arrows](/support/slides/draw-shapes-and-arrows)). Drag a thumbnail to reorder the slides ([Add, duplicate, and reorder slides](/support/slides/add-slides)). A background can be a color, a gradient or an image, for one slide or all of them ([Change a slide's background](/support/slides/slide-backgrounds)).

Slides runs on the same canvas as eigen|vector⟩, so shapes have the same hand-drawn look, which you can turn down to clean lines ([Style shapes, text, and images](/support/slides/style-objects)).

## From a sheet to a slide

Copy a chart in eigen|sheets⟩ and paste it on a slide, and it stays a chart you can edit, with the numbers it showed ([Add a chart to a spreadsheet](/support/sheets/add-a-chart), [Insert text and images](/support/slides/insert-content)). Give it the hand-drawn look and font, then Convert to shapes splits it into slices and labels you move one by one. An arrow docked on a slice moves with it ([Draw shapes, lines, and arrows on a slide](/support/slides/draw-shapes-and-arrows)).

## Presenting

Press Present and the slide fills the screen. Click or use the arrow keys to move on; a presentation clicker works too. Escape takes you back to the editor ([Present your slides](/support/slides/present)).

## Sharing and comments

You share a deck with people, a team or a link, and anyone with edit access can leave a comment on a text box, an image or a shape ([Share a presentation and comment](/support/slides/share-and-comment)).

## PowerPoint files

You can bring a `.pptx` in, as a new deck or as extra slides in the one you have open. Text, shapes, pictures, charts and backgrounds come across. Tables do not yet: each becomes a box that says what was there ([Import a PowerPoint presentation](/support/slides/import-powerpoint)). You download any deck as PowerPoint, PDF or a web page ([Export a presentation](/support/slides/export-slides)).
