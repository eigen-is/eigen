## For your technical person

- Runs with Docker on a Linux box.
- Installs with one line: `curl -fsSL https://eigen.is/install | sh`.
- Speaks IMAP, SMTP, CalDAV, CardDAV and WebDAV, so the apps already on your phone and computer work with it.
- Open source under the MIT license.
- Pre-1.0: expect changes between releases.
- Read more in [Self-hosting](/support/self-hosting).
