eigen|chat⟩ is the messaging app in Eigen. Your rooms are in the sidebar, your own at the top and your teams' below them, and you type at the bottom of the room ([Get started with Chat](/support/chat/get-started)). It is plain text, files and a few slash commands.

## Rooms for people and for teams

A personal room lives in your own Drive, and you decide who reads it and who writes in it. A team room belongs to the team, so every member sees it and access follows who is in the team. ([Personal chats and team chats](/support/chat/personal-and-team-chats)). If you start a chat with exactly the people you already have a room with, that room opens instead of a second one ([Create a chat room](/support/chat/create-room)).

## As it happens

A message shows up for everyone in the room while they look at it ([Get started with Chat](/support/chat/get-started)). Type `@` and a list of the room's members appears to pick from ([Mention someone in a message](/support/chat/mentions)). A room you wrote in gets a dot when something new arrives, and the bell tells you about mentions, whispers and new messages in your rooms ([Unread indicators and notifications](/support/chat/notifications)). You can edit or delete your own messages. A deleted one is gone for good, files included ([Send, edit, and delete messages](/support/chat/send-messages)).

## Emotes and commands

Type `/` and Chat suggests what you can type. There are more than 80 emotes, such as `/groan`, `/highfive` and `/cheer`, and many take a person, which changes what everyone sees: Anouk reads "You thank Timo Jansen", Timo reads "Anouk de Wit thanks you" and the rest of the room "Anouk de Wit thanks Timo Jansen". `/me` writes your own, and `/whisper` sends a line only one person can read; the rest of the room sees a placeholder ([Chat commands](/support/chat/commands)).

## Files in a chat

The paperclip attaches a file from Drive or your computer, and you can paste an image straight in. A document, sheet or deck goes as a link, so whoever opens it needs access to it. A file you receive opens in a preview, saves to Drive, or turns into a sheet, document or deck if it came as Excel, Word or PowerPoint ([Share files in a chat](/support/chat/share-files)).
