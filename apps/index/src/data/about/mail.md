eigen|mail⟩ is the email app in Eigen. Your mailboxes on the left, the list in the middle, the open message on the right ([Get started with Mail](/support/mail/get-started)). It is ordinary mail on your own server, so the mail app on your phone can read it too.

## Reading and replying

A reply quotes the message it answers, so the last mail in a thread carries the whole conversation. Pictures a message loads from the internet stay hidden until you click Load images, so a sender can't tell when you read it. Reply, Reply all and Forward only open a draft: nothing goes out until you click Send ([Read and reply to email](/support/mail/read-and-reply)).

The sidebar holds six mailboxes, with any folders you made in another mail app below them ([Get started with Mail](/support/mail/get-started)).

## Attachments land in Drive

Click an attachment to preview it. A contact file reads as its cards and a calendar file as its events. From there you save it to a folder in Drive, or turn an `.xlsx` into a sheet, a `.docx` into a document or a `.pptx` into slides, and the copy opens straight away ([Save an attachment to Drive](/support/mail/save-attachments)).

It works the other way too. Attach a file from Drive, and a document goes as a link rather than a copy ([Attach files to an email](/support/mail/attach-files)). If someone on the email can't open it, Mail asks whether to share it before it sends ([Give access to a document you link in an email](/support/mail/share-a-linked-document)).

## Finding mail

The box above the list filters the folder you're in, by sender, recipients, subject and text. The search at the top of the window looks through all your mail at once, and `from:` and `to:` narrow it to a person ([Search your email](/support/mail/search-your-email)). An invitation from someone's calendar shows as a card in the message, with a button that opens it in Calendar ([Get started with Mail](/support/mail/get-started)).

## In the mail app on your phone

Your mailbox speaks IMAP and SMTP, so Thunderbird, Apple Mail or another mail app can read and send from it, beside the browser or instead of it ([Set up Eigen Mail in a mail client](/support/connect/mail-client)). That includes the apps on your phone ([Connect external apps to Eigen](/support/connect/overview)). Each app gets its own app password, which you can revoke without touching your main one ([Create and manage app passwords](/support/connect/app-passwords)).
