eigen|vector⟩ is the drawing app in Eigen. It is a whiteboard on an infinite canvas: boxes, arrows, freehand lines, text and images, with no page edge to bump into ([Get started](/support/vector/get-started)). It is heavily inspired by [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com/), but not a fork.

::: video src="/data/about/media/vector-hero" caption="The main stage moved, and the power line and the crowd flow follow it."
:::

## Arrows that stay attached

Start an arrow on one shape and end it on another, and it docks to both. Move, resize or rotate a shape, and the arrow follows. An arrow can be straight, curved or an elbow that routes around shapes at right angles, and it can carry a label ([Connect objects with arrows and lines](/support/vector/connect-with-arrows)).

## The hand-drawn look

Everything is drawn with a slightly wobbly line, as if by hand. Fills can be hatched. The sketch style goes from Architect, which is clean, to Cartoonist, the roughest ([Style shapes, lines, text, and images](/support/vector/style-objects)).

## From a sheet to a drawing

Copy a chart in eigen|sheets⟩ and paste it on a drawing, and it stays a chart you can edit, with the numbers it showed ([Add a chart to a spreadsheet](/support/sheets/add-a-chart), [Draw shapes, freehand lines, and text](/support/vector/draw-shapes-and-text)). Give it the hand-drawn look and font, then Convert to shapes splits it into slices and labels you move one by one. An arrow docked on a slice moves with it ([Connect objects with arrows and lines](/support/vector/connect-with-arrows)).

## Moving around

You pan by scrolling and zoom from 10% to 3000%. ⌘F finds text anywhere on the canvas, also in the parts you scrolled away from ([Zoom, pan, find, and keyboard shortcuts](/support/vector/zoom-pan-and-shortcuts)).

## Sharing and export

You share a drawing with people, a team or a link, and leave comments on a single shape or on the drawing as a whole ([Share a drawing and comment](/support/vector/share-and-comment)). A drawing downloads as an SVG image or a PDF, cropped to what you drew. ([Download a drawing as SVG or PDF](/support/vector/export-drawing)).
