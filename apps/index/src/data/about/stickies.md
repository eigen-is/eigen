eigen|stickies⟩ is the [kanban board](/support/stickies/get-started) in Eigen. Columns run left to right, each holds cards, and every card has a title, a description, a color and its own comment thread.

:::

## Columns and cards

The festival crew has one board with three columns: to do, doing and done. A card is a job, such as Order fencing or Print wristbands. Where it sits says how far it is.

[Drag a card](/support/stickies/move-cards) to another column, or up and down in its own, and Ctrl+Z puts it back if you got it wrong. Hold Alt while you drag and you leave a copy.

A description can hold a [checklist](/support/stickies/add-and-edit-cards), and the card shows how far along it is. Order fencing has three steps and says 2/3: the quotes are in, the delivery slot is confirmed, and the final quote waits for a signature.

Columns [rename, reorder and delete](/support/stickies/column-settings), and undo brings a deleted column back.

## A thread on every card

Open a card and [its thread](/support/stickies/card-comments) is under the description. The card face counts the replies, and a red dot says something in it is new for you. The link icon copies a link that lands straight on the card.

On the Sunday wind cover card Timo asks whether the second stage needs a tent. Saar answers that they decide at the go/no-go meeting. Later she adds the quote from the tent hire, with their contact card attached. Whoever opens the card reads the whole story in order.

A card also [carries files](/support/stickies/attach-files-to-a-card): an image becomes its cover, and a document goes on as a link rather than a copy.

## Who does what

Right-click a card and pick someone under [Assign to](/support/stickies/assign-and-resolve-cards). They get a notification, and at most one email an hour per board. Resolve a card when it is done; it stays where it is with a tick.

The [Filter menu](/support/stickies/filter-a-board) narrows the board to one person, a few colors or what is still open. So Timo can look at his own cards and nothing else.

## Sharing a board

A board is private until you [share it](/support/stickies/share-board), with people, a team or a link. Everyone with edit access works on it at the same time, and a card someone else drags moves on your screen too.

Like a document, a board keeps a [version history](/support/drive/file-versions) you can restore from.
