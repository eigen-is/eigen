eigen|stickies⟩ is the kanban board in Eigen. Columns run left to right, each holds cards, and every card has a title, a description, a color and its own comment thread ([Get started with Stickies](/support/stickies/get-started)). It is a board of sticky notes, and that is all it tries to be.

## Columns and cards

Drag a card to another column, or up and down in its own, and Ctrl+Z puts it back if you got it wrong ([Move and reorder cards](/support/stickies/move-cards)). A description can hold a checklist, and the card shows how far along it is, such as 2/3. Hold Alt while you drag and you leave a copy ([Add and edit cards](/support/stickies/add-and-edit-cards)). Columns rename, reorder and delete, and undo brings a deleted column back while the board is still open ([Customize columns](/support/stickies/column-settings)).

## A thread on every card

Open a card and its thread is under the description. The card face counts the replies, and a red dot says something in it is new for you. The link icon copies a link that lands straight on the card ([Discuss a card with comments](/support/stickies/card-comments)). A card also carries files: an image becomes its cover, and a document goes on as a link rather than a copy ([Attach files to a card](/support/stickies/attach-files-to-a-card)).

## Who does what

Right-click a card and pick someone under Assign to. They get a notification, and at most one email an hour per board. Resolve a card when it is done; it stays where it is with a tick ([Assign and resolve cards](/support/stickies/assign-and-resolve-cards)). The Filter menu narrows the board to one person, a few colors or what is still open. It only changes what you see, and it isn't saved ([Filter the cards on a board](/support/stickies/filter-a-board)).

## Sharing a board

A board is private until you share it, with people, a team or a link. A link still asks for a sign-in; there is no anonymous sharing ([Share a board and manage access](/support/stickies/share-board)). Like a document, a board keeps a version history you can restore from ([See and restore previous versions of a file](/support/drive/file-versions)).
